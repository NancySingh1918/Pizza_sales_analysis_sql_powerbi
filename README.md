# Pizza_sales_analysis_sql_powerbi
End-to-end pizza sales analysis using SQL and Power BI to analyze KPIs, sales trends, and top- and bottom-performing pizzas through an interactive dashboard.

🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis | SQL & Power BI

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pizza sales data using SQL and Microsoft Power BI to identify sales trends, evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), and uncover insights into product performance.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from querying sales data with SQL to transforming business metrics into an interactive Power BI dashboard.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance using key business metrics.
- Understand daily and monthly order trends.
- Evaluate sales contribution by pizza category and size.
- Identify the best- and worst-performing pizzas.
- Present insights through interactive and easy-to-understand visualizations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- SQL — Data querying, aggregation, filtering, grouping, and ranking.
- Microsoft Power BI — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- Data Analytics — KPI analysis and business insights.

🔍 SQL Analysis

SQL queries were used to answer important business questions, including:

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. What is the average order value?
3. How many pizzas were sold?
4. How many unique orders were placed?
5. What is the average number of pizzas per order?
6. Which days and months have the highest order volumes?
7. What percentage of revenue comes from each pizza category?
8. How does revenue vary by pizza size?
9. Which five pizzas generate the highest revenue?
10. Which five pizzas generate the lowest revenue?
11. Which pizzas perform best and worst by quantity sold and total orders?

SQL techniques used include:

- "SUM()", "COUNT()" and "CAST()"
- "GROUP BY" and "ORDER BY"
- "WHERE" filtering
- "DISTINCT"
- Subqueries
- "LIMIT"
- Date functions such as "DAYNAME()" and "MONTHNAME()"

📈 Power BI Dashboard

The project contains two dashboard pages:

1. Sales Overview

- KPI cards for overall sales performance.
- Daily and monthly order trends.
- Revenue contribution by pizza category.
- Sales distribution by pizza size.
- Pizza quantities sold by category.

2. Best & Worst Sellers

- Top five pizzas by revenue.
- Top five pizzas by quantity sold.
- Top five pizzas by total orders.
- Bottom five pizzas across the same performance measures.

The dashboard also includes filters for pizza category and order date to support interactive exploration.

💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard's full-year overview:

- Classic category: Contributes the highest share of sales and total orders.
- Large pizza size: Generates the highest revenue contribution.
- Friday: Records the highest number of orders among the days displayed.
- January and July: Show peak monthly order volumes.
- Top revenue performers: Thai Chicken and Barbecue Chicken pizzas rank among the leading products by revenue.
- Lowest revenue performer: The Brie Carre Pizza appears among the bottom performers by revenue, quantity, and total orders.

These findings can help a business understand product performance, sales patterns, and areas that may need further investigation.
